## SUMMARY STATISTICS

### Overall Accuracy
- ✅ **Correctly Assessed**: 5/10 (50%)
- ⚠️ **Partially Correct**: 3/10 (30%)
- ❌ **Incorrect**: 2/10 (20%)

### Issues Found

1. **Persona Mismatch (3 posts)**:
   - POST 2: Deep personal growth → assigned qronoya (should be aspandead)
   - POST 3: Deep personal growth → assigned qronoya (should be aspandead)
   - POST 7: Random news → assigned qronoya (should be none/low)

2. **Low Quality Content (2 posts)**:
   - POST 7: Random news aggregation - value too high (5.0)
   - POST 9: Conspiracy/hoax content - value way too high (7.0)

3. **Persona Score Issues (1 post)**:
   - POST 10: Crypto content but persona score only 1.0 (should be higher for claimzilla)

### Root Causes

1. **PersonaMatcher not working correctly for personal growth content**
   - Deep philosophical/personal growth content is being assigned to qronoya instead of aspandead
   - PersonaMatcher may not be recognizing emotional depth signals properly

2. **Value scoring too lenient**
   - Low-quality content (random aggregation, conspiracy) getting scores of 5.0-7.0
   - Need better quality filters

3. **Content reliability not being checked**
   - Conspiracy/hoax content not being flagged
   - Need reliability/credibility scoring

4. **Persona score normalization issue**
   - POST 10: Crypto content should have higher claimzilla score
   - PersonaMatcher may be returning low scores that aren't being normalized correctly

### Recommendations

1. **Fix PersonaMatcher for personal growth content**
   - Improve detection of emotional depth, philosophy, mindfulness signals
   - Ensure aspandead gets these posts

2. **Add content quality filters**
   - Detect random aggregation (multiple unrelated topics)
   - Detect conspiracy/hoax content
   - Lower value scores for low-quality content

3. **Add reliability scoring**
   - Flag unreliable sources/content
   - Exclude from usable_posts if reliability too low

4. **Review PersonaMatcher scoring**
   - Check why crypto content gets low scores
   - Ensure proper normalization to 0-10 scale
